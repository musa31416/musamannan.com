    I am a Bangladeshi author, researcher and educator. This archive follows the events surrounding my attempts to keep writing and speaking publicly. The Facebook takedowns divide the chapters; the story also includes threats, attacks on other platforms, university complaints, and targeting of people close to me.

    Platform notices, public claims, support exchanges and my testimony are identified separately. A group’s claim of responsibility is evidence of what it asserted; it does not independently reveal Meta’s internal decisions. Historical captions retain the source author’s language, including allegations and interpretations. The source index provides provenance; full underlying documents remain private.

    The eighth account’s appeal was submitted on October 28, 2026. Its last supplied notice shows review pending. This page does not imply that the appeal has since been decided.

    05 / Provenance and scope

    What the evidence establishes

    Dates and account status

    Facebook’s dated notices directly establish losses of accounts 2, 3, 4 and 6. The first and fifth dates are supported by the author’s historical record. The seventh date follows the author’s corrected chronology and the preserved review reply. The eighth is supported by the author’s account and a dated appeal confirmation. A suspension, an appeal and permanent disablement are distinct events.

    Originals and contextual association

    Images are extracted from the supplied records or copied from original screenshot files. Exact byte duplicates are merged with multiple source references. Originals are not enhanced or rewritten. Small browser previews are generated for loading speed; the viewer and download links use the originals. Chapter association provides reading context; ambiguous evidence is kept in the shared collection. A file’s name or modification time is not its event date.

    Allegations and source captions

    Testimony, disputed quotations, translations and third-party claims remain attributed to their source. Historical captions may use stronger language than the chapter narrative. They are preserved as descriptions written in the underlying records, not independently verified conclusions.

    A public archive with a private source collection

Banking statements, identity documents, private identifying correspondence and litigation-preparation notes are retained outside the public site. The public source index does not reproduce the full underlying documents or OCR transcriptions. Some records still need precise dates or further corroboration; the archive remains open to documented corrections.
