    Using Controlled In-Plane Lattice Strain to Tune Charge Transfer Between Monolayer MoS2 and Adsorbed Dyes

    Musa Ibne Mannan29 October 2026Draft for discussion7 figures · 25 references

    Pressure, doping and alloying have all been used to tune how charge moves between a dye and a 2D semiconductor, but none of them isolates the substrate. We propose a Raman-compatible bending stage that strains CVD-grown MoS2 by a set, measured amount while the dye layer stays untouched, and test whether dye charge transfer follows the strain.
