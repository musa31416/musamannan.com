The Huxley–Imon Classification

Nine people can carry the same label and stand in nine different places.

A label a person picks for themself can't place them. Pick a label below to meet nine people who all claim it. Each one holds their position differently (method) and needs God to a different degree (need), so each stands on a different cell of the floor. The label only sets the height.

Pick a label

Person

    Lens One · Method

    Did the position come before the reasoning, or after it?

    Theist: holds that there is a God before any reasoning.
    Agnostic: reasons first, whatever the conclusion, and would revise it if the reasons changed.
    Atheist: holds a position without God before any reasoning.

    Lens Two · Identity

    What does the person call themself? Identity is recorded as a tag (the height of the pin) and is never used to place the person on the floor.

    Lens Three · Need

    How often does the person need God, for any reason at all?

    God-dependent: always.
    God-situational: sometimes, in crisis or when it pays.
    God-independent: never.

    Adapted from The Huxley–Imon Classification of Homo sapiens: Identity–Need–Method Definitions of Atheism, by Kishor Pasha Imon (aka Musa Ibne Mannan), November 2026. The people described here are illustrative composites; placing a real person is an inference from everything known about how that person holds their position.
