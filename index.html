Selected work

Projects

Four representative pieces. Each one is written up in more detail in the CV, and full project pages are on the way.

Machine gun barrel thermal and stress failure

Transient thermal and von Mises stress analysis of a barrel under sustained high-rate firing, of the kind that forced frequent barrel changes on WWII weapons firing up to 1,200 rounds per minute. Chamber pressure was worked out analytically at roughly 130.5 MPa, then AISI 4140, 316L and ASTM A656 Grade 7 were compared for thermal gradient and stress concentration. AISI 4140 showed the lowest peak von Mises stress of the three.

AbaqusTransient heat transferMaterial selection

Cantilever beam FEA and mesh convergence study

A 10 m cantilever under 2,000 N shear, analyzed with CPS4, CPS8 and C3D8 elements across coarse, medium and fine meshes. Maximum deflection error in 3D fell from about 84 percent to 22 percent; 2D stress error at mid-span fell from over 94 percent to 8.6 percent. Independently verified with custom MATLAB code using 2×2 Gauss quadrature, which confirmed the accuracy penalty of single-point integration.

AbaqusMATLABGauss quadratureVerification

Ragweed-PLA biocomposite failure diagnosis

Harvested and retted giant ragweed fiber, treated it with AMEO and GLYMO silanes, extruded filament on a twin-screw extruder and printed test coupons by FDM. EDAX confirmed silicon at the fiber surface and SEM showed no fiber-matrix detachment. GLYMO-treated composite reached 54.24 MPa average flexural strength. The honest finding was that fiber aspect ratio varied from 2:1 to 8:1, which limits repeatability.

Twin-screw extrusionFDMSEM / EDAXTGA / DSC

Multi-objective energy plan optimization

A MATLAB framework using multi-objective genetic algorithms over ERCOT residential usage data from 2002 to 2022, balancing cost against renewable share and cancellation fees. Converged after 101 generations. Mostly interesting as a demonstration that evolutionary methods handle genuinely competing objectives without collapsing them into one weighted score.
